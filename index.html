Good Fit / Not The Thing

Built for businesses with substance.

This works best when you have real experience, a business worth sharpening, and enough trust to let the heavy creative work happen before the review window.

Good Fit

  • You want strategy, messaging, identity, and a simple website to work together.
  • You can give the project a few focused windows of real attention.
  • You like direct feedback and a decisive creative partner.

Not The Thing

  • Ongoing e-commerce support or complicated custom development.
  • Logo-only work with no positioning or message direction.
  • A live design jam where the brand is invented from scratch while everyone watches.

About

Brand strategy for businesses with too much substance to blend in.

Big Bad Branding is led by Jillian Noble, the designer behind Elbongurk. After years building identities, packaging, websites, and visual systems, I created Big Bad for a more focused kind of project: strategy-led brands and websites for businesses that are ready to look and sound like themselves.

The Person Behind It

Strategy brain. Designer hands.

I’ve spent years building brands, packaging, websites, and visual systems for businesses that needed more than a pretty logo. The work I care about most is figuring out what a business is really trying to say, then making it visible.

What I’m Good At

Seeing the thing under the thing.

The half-formed positioning. The better headline hiding in the messy explanation. The reason people should care. The visual direction that makes the business feel more like itself.

How I Work

Direct, strategic, and allergic to busywork.

I am not here to make you a pile of options and ask you to guess. We make a plan, use it as the creative brief, and build from a point of view.

Who This Helps

People with the goods, but not the translation.

You already have experience, taste, opinions, and something worth saying. The brand just needs to catch up so the right people can recognize it faster.

Why Big Bad Exists

You are not too much. Your brand is saying too little.

Good businesses often end up looking safer and more generic than they actually are. Big Bad exists to make the substance obvious.

The problem

  • Your business has depth, but your site sounds like everyone else.
  • You keep explaining what makes you different.
  • The logo, copy, and website are not pulling in the same direction.

The better feeling

  • People understand what you do faster.
  • Your brand feels braver without feeling fake.
  • The business finally looks and sounds like the real deal it already is.

Point Of View

The brand is not the costume. It is the clue.

A logo matters, but only because it points to a bigger feeling, memory, and expectation. The real work is deciding what people should understand about you before they even talk to you.

Background

Branding, packaging, websites, and the words that make them work.

My work has crossed identity systems, food and beverage packaging, service-business websites, medical and wellness brands, and strategy-first creative direction through Elbongurk and now Big Bad Branding.

The Method

The strategy comes first for a reason.

When the strategy is fuzzy, every design decision gets harder. The written plan gives the work a spine before the identity, message, and website get built.

The Promise

This is not about making you louder. It is about making you clearer.

The work should build people up, not flatten them out. You are the real deal. The brand just needs to start acting like it.

You already have the substance. Let’s make the brand brave enough to show it.
